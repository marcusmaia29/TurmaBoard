# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in TurmaBoard a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Prioritize the health of the community over individual preferences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate platform safety concerns, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Send
project-specific reports to
[vinicius99maia@gmail.com](mailto:vinicius99maia@gmail.com). Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue.

Project maintainers will review reports fairly and as confidentially as
possible. They may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a contributor when appropriate. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
