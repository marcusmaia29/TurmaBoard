# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are undergraduate students checking shared academic deadlines, links, and course notes between classes, often from a phone. A shared administrator account maintains the board while visitors can read all published academic information without signing in.

## Product Purpose

TurmaBoard is the fastest shared reference for understanding what the class must deliver, when it is due, and where the official source lives. Success means a first-time visitor can find the current week's priorities within seconds and an administrator can update them without training.

## Positioning

One reliable class board replaces the repeated search across chats, learning platforms, email, and separate personal calendars without pretending to replace official course sources.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and collaborative. The interface should feel dependable and lightweight, with direct Brazilian Portuguese copy and no institutional stiffness.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense enterprise dashboards, personal kanban workflows, decorative animation, competing accent colors, hidden primary actions, and long text inside overview cards.

## Design Principles

- Put this week's deadlines before secondary context.
- Store information once and present it consistently across every view.
- Make urgency and provenance legible without creating alarm fatigue.
- Keep contribution flows short while preserving a transparent audit trail.
- Preserve full usability on small mobile screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Do not rely on color alone for course, type, urgency, or status. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep Portuguese labels concise and explicit.
